- **Duration**: 25-30 seconds
- **Audio**: Fast-tempo lo-fi or trending tech synth
- **Visuals**:

  - 0:04 - 0:12: Instant switch to NEXUS Excel sheet: instant click, habit checkbox checked, progress bar jumps to 85%, streak badge glows green.
  - 0:12 - 0:22: Rapid unboxing scroll: Daily Planner -> 20-Goal Matrix -> Finance Tracker -> Sunday Review.
  - 0:22 - 0:30: Text: "16-in-1 System. 100% Offline. ₹10 / $1 one-time. Link in bio."

### Format B: "Sunday Reset Routine" (Aesthetic ASMR)
- **Duration**: 20-25 seconds
- **Audio**: Keyboard typing ASMR / cozy chillhop
- **Visuals**:
  - Close-up shot opening `Personal_Growth_System.xlsx` on Sunday evening.
  - Typing in weekly goals, checking off workout habits.
  - Highlighting the automated weekly analytics chart.
  - Text: "How I organize my entire week in 7 minutes without recurring subscriptions."
